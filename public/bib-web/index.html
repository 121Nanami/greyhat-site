The world

Above us, only angels

The angels that descended on Tokyo are not the ones from scripture. They are beings of pure light and mathematics, perfect things built to kill everything. Everyone left on the surface died.

What remains of humanity lives underground in the arc. To fight back, it built operators: autonomous, sentient soldiers who are part human, part machine and part angel.

The government that commands them is mostly a dictatorship. It sells the operators' fights for sponsorship money and public support. You play as a commander, following Luna, Plack and Kuu as they take government jobs, fight angels and deal with other operators' problems.

How it plays

Three operators. One on the field.

A 3D action game with full movement: run, climb, swim, block, parry and dodge across Neo Tokyo and the arc.

Squad

Switch on the fly

Bring three operators into every level. One fights at a time, and you can swap the active operator with a single button. If your active operator falls, the next in line steps in.

Kit

Five ability slots

Every operator, from Rare to Secret, has the same core layout. Legendary and Secret operators also carry passives.

Campaign

Clear every section

Talk your way into each battle, then clear 10 to 30 enemies per section to push forward. Every level has a mini-boss, and every chapter ends with a boss.

Summoning

Rates, in the open

One Epic or better in every 10-pull. A guaranteed 5★ within 100 summons and a guaranteed 6★ within 2,000. Lose a featured 50/50 and the next one is yours.

Every non-daily summon also earns Mileage, which carries across banners and can be spent on the featured operator.

FAQ

When does Boldinbeauty release?

There is no release date yet. The game is in active development. Follow the news section or our socials for updates.

Which platforms is it coming to?

Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.

What kind of game is it?

A 3D action gacha game. You collect operators through summoning, build a team of three and fight through a story set in Neo Tokyo between 2009 and 2017.

Who is making it?

Boldinbeauty is an independent project built in Unreal Engine 5.

How can I help?

Following the project and sharing it helps a lot. You can support development on Patreon or Buy Me a Coffee from the support page, and official merch is on the way.
